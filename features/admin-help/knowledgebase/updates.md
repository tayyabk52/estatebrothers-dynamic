# Updates Admin Guide

## Purpose

Updates are articles, announcements, and market notes. They should be useful, dated, and authored clearly.

## Important fields

- Title: clear article headline.
- Slug: URL-friendly article path.
- Summary: short preview text.
- Body: useful full content.
- Author: person or Estate Brothers.
- Status: draft, review, published, or archived.
- Published date: when the update becomes public.
- Thumbnail/OG image: preview image.
- Meta title and description: search/social preview text.

## SEO rules

- Publish only complete updates.
- Make the article useful without requiring the admin dashboard.
- Use real author information.
- Keep image alt text descriptive.
- Do not publish placeholder market notes.
